Genre: Espionage Thriller Language: Malayalam Director: Mahesh Narayanan Cast: Mammootty, Mohanlal, Fahadh Faasil, Nayanthara, and others. Plot: The film follows a government official who uncovers a massive surveillance conspiracy involving high-ranking political figures. He is forced to go on the run to expose the truth. It is notable for reuniting superstars Mammootty and Mohanlal on screen.
